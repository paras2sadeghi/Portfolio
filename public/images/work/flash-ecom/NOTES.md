# Flash Pharmacy: Local Design Captures

Captured on the user's Windows computer on 20 October 2026 from `http://localhost:5173/`.

## Capture Method

- Playwright with the installed Microsoft Edge Chromium browser, in fresh, disposable browser contexts.
- Desktop: 1440 x 900 CSS pixels, deviceScaleFactor 2.
- Phone: 390 x 844 CSS pixels, deviceScaleFactor 3, isMobile and hasTouch enabled.
- Pages were scrolled incrementally before their top viewport was captured. Long pages also have `-full.jpg` captures. A bounded wait allowed slow assets to settle without waiting indefinitely.
- JPG exports use quality 85. Exports are downsampled to keep the complete folder below approximately 15 MB: no desktop export exceeds 2400 pixels wide and no phone export exceeds 1170 pixels. The image manifest records each final size. Repetitive catalog pages are deliberately smaller than the featured case-study images.
- Cookie consent was dismissed through the Essential only control after recording the initial notice. No application source, design, or CSS was changed to create these views.
- Only the screenshots, this note and their manifest are intended as repository changes. Capture tooling and intermediate evidence were kept outside the portfolio checkout.

## How To Use The Files

- `home.jpg` and `mobile-home.jpg`: main entry screen; `home-full.jpg`: long homepage.
- `category-*.jpg`: main product categories; `shop-*.jpg`: broader departments. `mobile-` versions show the phone layout.
- `product-detail*`: featured CeraVe cleanser. `product-makeup*`: NARS blush. `product-vitamin*`: Jamieson vitamin. `product-out-of-stock*`: Cetaphil stock state.
- `product-detail-shade-selected*`, `product-detail-size-selected*`, `product-shade-guidance*`, `filter-*`, `sort-*`, `category-filter-applied*`, `cart*` and `checkout*`: meaningful interaction states. Phone equivalents use `mobile-` prefixes.
- `route-*`: supplementary captures for individual product and subcategory routes, rather than just one representative catalog template.
- `manifest.json`: exact filenames, routes, viewport configuration and final pixel dimensions.
- The generated tables below list the actual captures and every discovered link. A link being listed does not establish that a transaction or authentication flow was completed.

## Experience And Interface Copy

### Home And Discovery

Headline: **Healthcare for your whole family.** Desktop copy describes online doctors, prescriptions, everyday health and beauty, same-day delivery, and Flash Longevity+. Primary links are **Fill a prescription** and **Talk to a doctor**.

Sections include **Pharmacist pick of the day.**, **Shop by category**, **Popular in makeup.**, **Your very own Smart Pharmacist.**, **Shop, earn, glow.**, **What our clients say.**, **Trendy on social media.**, **Know your biological age.**, and **Got questions?**

Observed interactions include the announcement text rotation, horizontally moving brand logos, product/category carousel controls, scroll-triggered content reveals, social-video tiles, FAQ expansion, biological-age comparison controls and a floating Ask Flash entry. Screenshots are still images, not recordings of the motion.

### Catalog, Search And Filters

Main categories include Cold & Flu, Vitamins & Supplements, Baby & Child, Just for Him, Skincare, Sexual Health, Mom-to-Be, Prescriptions, Makeup & Beauty, Personal Care, Pain Relief, Hair & Nails, Just for Her, and Senior Care.

Department labels are Shop all, Medicine, Vitamins, Beauty, Personal Care, and Baby & Family. Category pages show subcategory navigation, product grids, stock/discount badges, wishlist hearts and cart actions. Empty subcategories say **More [subcategory] arriving soon.** Parent-category titles can remain visible on subcategory routes.

The phone **Filters** sheet offers Brand, Availability and Price, **Clear all**, and an apply action such as **Show 2 products**. The sampled brand selection is Jamieson. **Sort by** offers Featured, Best sellers, Price: Low to High, and Price: High to Low. Applied filtering has a removable chip and a changed result count. These are ordinary catalog filters; no separate predictive "smart filters" capability was verified.

Search suggestions include products and categories. The sampled query is **vitamin**; **See all results for "vitamin"** opens `#/search/vitamin`. The results page has matching-category shortcuts and **Sort: Best match**.

### Product Details And Beauty Guidance

Common controls include **Add to bag**, **Buy now**, quantity controls, Share, wishlist, availability and fulfillment options. An **Auto-replenish** switch displays an alternate price. Related/bundle recommendations and reviews appear below the main purchase area. No auto-replenishment subscription was activated.

The featured **Hydrating Facial Cleanser** has photo controls, size choices, **Why you'll love it**, **A closer look**, **Key ingredients**, **How to use**, **Full ingredient list**, **Sensitivity & skin type**, **Pairs well with**, and **Ratings & reviews**.

The **Insatiable Liquid Blush** page has named shade swatches, an expanded shade list, images of the shade on skin, application/ingredient content and reviews labeled by shade and skin tone. **Which shade suits my skin tone?** opens a product-specific pharmacist dialog titled **Ask about this product**, with conversational shade guidance and follow-up prompts. An **All skin tones** control is visible in reviews. Do not describe this as a verified camera-based skin-tone scanner or automated skin-tone matcher; no such scanning flow was observed.

Vitamin pages use sections such as **Why it may support you**, **Supplement Facts**, **Who it may be for**, **How to take**, **Warnings** and **Related vitamins**, depending on the SKU. These are rendered product claims, not independent medical verification.

The out-of-stock cleanser shows a notification action and alternative products. No notification request was submitted.

### Fulfillment, Bag And Demo Checkout

The product fulfillment UI offers **Delivery** and **Pickup**, an address entry action, pharmacy selection, and availability/cutoff copy. On phone it appears as a **Delivery or pickup** sheet. Pickup shows a ready-time message and selected pharmacy. No personal address was entered.

The bag is an overlay on its underlying route, not necessarily a separate cart URL. It has empty and populated states, selected variant/size, quantity and removal controls, save-for-later actions, Subtotal, a Glow Points earning message and **Checkout**.

`#checkout` explicitly says **Demo checkout: no payment is taken.** Its sections are **Contact**, **Delivery or pickup**, **Payment**, **Your items**, and **Order summary**. Visible controls include guest email/phone fields, **Sign in for faster checkout**, delivery-speed choices, Apple Pay/card choices, card number/expiry/CVC fields, **Add a promo code**, **Apply**, and **Place order**. Switching to Pickup changes the summary and removes delivery-speed selection. No order was placed and no payment/contact data was entered.

The captured bag uses a temporary non-prescription cleanser in an isolated browser session. It does not alter the user's existing open browser cart. Wishlist empty state says **Nothing saved yet** and explains saving with a product heart.

### Telehealth And Smart Pharmacist

`#/app/chat` offers **Book a doctor**, **Upload a prescription**, **Check a symptom**, **Refill**, **New chat**, attachment and message controls. The assistant displays a disclaimer that it explains and organises, does not diagnose or prescribe, and that a doctor reviews clinical matters.

`#/app/chat/book` opens appointment availability, with an earliest-available option, date and hour choices, **More dates**, and **Pick a time**. The Doctors screen includes **Talk to a doctor**, **Book a call**, and **Past calls**. No appointment was booked or held and no consultation was conducted.

The homepage `#booking` link currently starts symptom triage rather than the same doctor-availability screen. Its first step asks **Where is it?** with body-area choices. No personal symptom was submitted.

`#/app/chat/refill` shows medication/refill and renewal choices, including doctor/pharmacist provider options. The capture context was fresh and no personal medical data was entered; these screens must not be presented as evidence of a completed clinical service. No refill or prescription submission occurred. `#fill` asks the visitor to sign in before a prescription can be checked/filled.

### Longevity And Loyalty

`#join` and `#longevity` lead to the Flash for Teams program. Its headline is **The healthcare benefit your team will actually use.** Actions include **Book a demo**, **See how it works**, and **Preview sample report**.

Visible content includes a biomarker ticker, body-system groups, biological-age and health-trend illustrations, enrollment/labs/strategy steps, a comparison section, pricing and testimonials. WHOOP and Apple Health syncing are described in copy; a working integration was not demonstrated. The displayed program price and biomarker counts are page content, not independently confirmed contractual terms or measured outcomes.

The Me entry exposes a **Your health, today.** membership gate over a dashboard preview, with **Unlock for $25/mo** and **Already a member? Sign in**. No subscription was purchased and protected member screens were not bypassed.

Glow Points appears on the homepage, account entry, bag and checkout. It says **100 points for every $1 on OTC and beauty**. The visible tiers are **Soft Glow**, **Lux Glow** and **Supernova Glow**, illustrated with green, blue and black membership cards. **Join Glow Points, it's free** leads to sign-in. No points were redeemed. The tier rules and displayed earning amounts are interface copy, not independently audited program economics.

### Account, Support And Legal

Flash sign-in says **Welcome to Flash.** and **Sign in or create an account. No password needed.** It offers phone/email, Continue, Apple, Google and a shopper demo action. No credentials were entered.

Guest account offers sign-in, loyalty enrollment, Smart pharmacist, Help centre and About Flash Pharmacy. Orders has an empty/history surface appropriate to the fresh session; no real user order was created for this package.

About explains the pharmacy and its services. Locations lists Lonsdale, Kitsilano, Downtown and Mount Pleasant, with hours, map, service information and direction/phone links. Help offers **How can we help?**, search, topic tabs, FAQ rows and **Still need help?** actions. Privacy policy, Terms of use and Accessibility have legal tabs and section navigation.

## Unfinished, Placeholder Or Unverified Elements

- Some subcategories are empty and use arriving-soon copy; some category thumbnails use a repeated fallback symbol.
- Some product pages show no reviews, and some image/product sections look less complete than the featured examples.
- A homepage Smart Pharmacist link uses `#/chat`, which rendered a **This page is out of stock.** 404; the working assistant entry observed in navigation is `#/app/chat`.
- Several footer/social/service links use `#` rather than a distinct destination. They are not separate implemented screens.
- The homepage doctor CTA and dedicated appointment entry start different flows.
- Desktop and phone filtering/sorting controls differ; a visible control is not proof that every implementation works.
- Legal pages explicitly mark their text **Draft for legal review. This text will be replaced before launch.** Their accessibility/privacy statements are not proof of compliance.
- Checkout is explicitly a demo. Successful payment, delivery, authentication, wearable syncing, real prescription processing and clinical outcomes were not verified.
- Customer counts, reviews, consumer-study percentages, testimonials, prices and health numbers are displayed app content or examples, not portfolio performance metrics. Do not turn them into claims about the designer's impact.
- No Vercel authentication prompt was encountered during the capture. If one had appeared, the run was configured to stop rather than log in.
